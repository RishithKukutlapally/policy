# E8-S1 — Refund rules: pro-rata, free-look, admin fee

- **ID:** E8-S1
- **Epic:** E8 — Cancellation & refunds
- **Layer:** Domain
- **Group:** S4-A
- **Sprint:** 4
- **Depends on:** E5-S1
- **Traces:** AC-08, AC-19, NFR-01

## Description

Implement pure refund rules in `src/domain/refund_rules.py` driven by the rule set's `cancellation` block
(`method: PRO_RATA`, `free_look_days: 15`, `admin_fee: "250.00"`). Within the free-look period the full
premium is refunded; afterwards the refund is `premium × unused_days / term_days − admin_fee`, floored at
`0.00`, quantized to 0.01 `ROUND_HALF_UP`. `days_elapsed = (cancellation_date − effective_date).days`,
`term_days = (expiry_date − effective_date).days + 1`, `unused_days = term_days − days_elapsed`; the result is
quantized once at the end. Free look applies to new business only (`previous_policy_number` is null) and while
`days_elapsed ≤ free_look_days`. Golden policy: `TL-2026-000001`, TERM_LIFE v1, sum insured `8000000.00`, age 25,
term_years 10, non-smoker → premium `12000.00` (8000000.00 × 0.0015, E2-S1), term `2026-01-01`–`2026-12-31`.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. [AC-19] Free-look: premium `12000.00`, effective `2026-01-01`, expiry `2026-12-31`; cancelling on `2026-01-10` (9 days) or on the boundary `2026-01-16` (15 days) refunds `Decimal("12000.00")` with no admin fee and `refund_type = FREE_LOOK` _(category: functional)_
   - Verify: call `calculate_refund(...)` for both dates; assert amount `Decimal("12000.00")` and `refund_type == "FREE_LOOK"`; assert `admin_fee_applied == Decimal("0.00")`
2. [AC-08] Pro-rata: same policy cancelled on `2026-04-01` → 90 days elapsed, 275 unused of 365 → `12000.00 × 275 / 365 − 250.00` = `Decimal("8791.10")` (pro-rata part 9041.0958…), `refund_type = PRO_RATA` _(category: functional)_
   - Verify: call `calculate_refund` with `cancellation_date=date(2026, 4, 1)`; assert `unused_days == 275`, `term_days == 365`; assert amount `== Decimal("8791.10")` and type `PRO_RATA`
3. [AC-19] Day after free-look: cancelling on `2026-01-17` (16 days elapsed, 349 unused) refunds `12000.00 × 349 / 365 − 250.00` = `Decimal("11223.97")`; a renewal term (`previous_policy_number` set) with the same dates cancelled on `2026-01-10` gets no free look: `12000.00 × 356 / 365 − 250.00` = `Decimal("11454.11")` `PRO_RATA` _(category: functional)_
   - Verify: call `calculate_refund` with `date(2026, 1, 17)`; assert amount `== Decimal("11223.97")` and type `PRO_RATA`; assert the result differs from the free-look amount; call it for the renewal-term case and assert `11454.11` and `PRO_RATA`
4. [—] The refund never goes negative and is always a `Decimal` with exponent `-2`: premium `2500.00` with 30 unused days → `205.48 − 250.00` → `Decimal("0.00")`; no `float` appears in the module _(category: functional)_
   - Verify: call `calculate_refund` for that case and assert `Decimal("0.00")`; property-test 500 random (premium, dates) inputs and assert `0.00 <= refund <= premium` and exponent `-2`; run hook `premium-precision-check` and assert no findings
5. [AC-08] A `cancellation.method` other than `PRO_RATA`, or a cancellation date before `effective_date` or after `expiry_date`, raises a typed validation error; policies in `LAPSED`, `CANCELLED` or `RENEWED` raise `InvalidPolicyStateException` via the E5-S1 state machine _(category: functional)_
   - Verify: pass a rule-set fixture with method `SHORT_RATE` and assert the error; pass `2025-12-31` and `2027-01-01` and assert the error; pass a CANCELLED status and assert `InvalidPolicyStateException`
